Working of Bi-GRU model

When the training script asks you about the "7 days", it is asking about the Input Window (or "Lookback Window"), not the total size of the database.

Here is exactly how it works:

1. Training (Happens in 

prediction.py
 / when clicking the Retrain button)
During training, the neural network learns by looking at the entire 6-month historical dataset in thousands of sliding 7-day chunks. Imagine taking a "magnifying glass" that covers exactly 7 days.

The neural network places the magnifying glass at the very beginning of your 6-month CSV (Days 1–7). It looks at those 7 days, and tries to predict Days 8–10.
Then it slides the magnifying glass forward by 6 hours. Now it looks at Days 1.25–7.25, and tries to predict the next 3 days.
It slides forward again and again, thousands of times, until it reaches the end of the 6-month file.
It uses 85% of those thousands of snapshots to learn the patterns, and holds back the final 15% of the snapshots to strictly test itself (validation).
2. Inference / Predicting the Future (Happens on the Dashboard)
Once the model has learned the patterns from 6 months of sliding snapshots, it only needs the most recent 7 days of live data to generate a brand new forecast.

So, when the Dashboard syncs via FTP:

It grabs the Server Room dataset.
It slices off only the final 7 days from that CSV (which equals 28 6-hour slots).
It hands those 7 days to the fully-trained BiGRU.
The BiGRU says: "Ah, I remember studying thousands of 7-day patterns looking just like this during training! Based on my entire life experience, here is what the next 3 days will look like."
Summary: The "7 days" is just the size of the chunk it needs to read at any given moment to make a prediction. But to become smart, it trained on all 6 months of data chopped into thousands of overlapping 7-day chunks!
